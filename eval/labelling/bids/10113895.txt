Offsite Continuous Risk Assessment (OCRA) refers to the 
continuous or periodic assessment of a bank’s risk profile 
using data, reports, transaction information and risk 
indicators available centrally, without depending solely on 
physical branch inspections. 
OCRA can be designed as a technology-enabled risk 
surveillance and early-warning mechanism to identify 
emerging risks across branches, administrative offices, 
business units and central operations.
