--- (p.9) Broad Scope of Work 
 
Bank intends to avail Dark Web Monitoring services for 03 (three) years. The details 
of Dark Web services include the following. 
Compromised Credentials & Sensitive Data Monitoring 
 
1. The service provider should be able to recognize and report Breached Usernames 
and passwords over different websites/ apps/ portals corresponding to UCO Bank 
which have been extracted from third parties or Bank itself. 
2. The service provider should be able to report any Personally Identifiable 
Information (PII) and data leaked pertaining to UCO Bank's customers/ employees. 
3. Services should be capable of identifying and reporting Personal accounts of key 
people — such as board members or top managers which criminals could have 
breached. The exact/tentative nos. of personal accounts will be shared with 
successful bidder. 
4. The service provider should have the capability of identifying sensitive 
document/information pertaining to customer/employees' credential and bank 
debit/credit cards with full details including complete card number being 
available on internet. 
5. The service should require with continuous scanning and monitoring, notifying the 
bank about any critical alert that puts our business, employees, or clients via dark 
web data leakage of customer/employees, credentials and bank debit/credit 
cards full details for taking necessary actions. 
6. The service provider should be able to continuously monitor deep and dark 
channels to provide Bank early detection of information leakage, contextual 
analysis of cyber-attacks and actionable alerts. 
7. The service provider should be to detect any sharing of customer information, 
account credentials of employees and customers which puts brand and business 
at risk. 
8. The proposed service provider must Safeguard the business against data leakage, 
breaches and the illegal selling of data on a broad range of deep and dark web 
sites including TOR, I2P, ZeroNet and paste Sites.
