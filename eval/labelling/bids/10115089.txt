--- (p.13) 2.71 Broad Scope of Work Early Warning System (EWS) for Loan Fraud 
Management 
2.71.1 Loan Portfolio Monitoring The solution shall monitor various loan portfolios of the Bank, 
including Agriculture, MSME, Retail, Housing, Vehicle, Personal Loans, Cash Credit, Overdraft, 
Term Loans and other secured and unsecured advances. 
2.71.2 Identification of Early Warning Signals 
a. The system shall identify early warning indicators based on borrower profile, loan account 
operations, transaction behaviour, repayment patterns, financial performance and other relevant 
information. 
b. The system shall support identification of individual warning indicators as well as combinations of 
multiple indicators that may indicate potential fraud. 
c. The solution shall provide a comprehensive set of standard EWS indicators and facilitate 
configuration of additional Bank-specific indicators as required. 
2.71.3 Transaction and Account Behaviour Monitoring 
The system shall identify suspicious activities and abnormal transaction patterns in loan accounts, 
including: 
a. Sudden and unusual withdrawals or utilisation of sanctioned limits. 
b. Diversion or siphoning of loan funds to unrelated accounts or third parties. 
c. Routing of funds through multiple accounts without apparent business justification. 
d. Large-value transactions inconsistent with the borrower's business profile. 
e. Frequent transfer of funds to related parties, associated entities or suspicious beneficiaries. 
f. Unusual cash withdrawals or transactions immediately after disbursement of loan amounts. 
g. Sudden decline in credits, business turnover or account activity. 
h. Transactions indicating possible diversion of funds for purposes other than the sanctioned loan 
purpose. 
i. Unusual fund movements between borrower accounts maintained within the Bank, wherever such 
information is available.
