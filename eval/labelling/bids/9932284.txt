Indian Overseas Bank invites bids from Original Equipment Manufacturers/ Original 
Software Developers having both ATM Switch & DCMS application for the Supply, 
Installation, Integration, Upgradation, Implementation , Migration and Maintenance 
of Hardware, Software, Network Links , Facility Management Services of ATM Switch 
& DCMS under OPEX Model for a period of 5 (Five) years from the date of 
acceptance of Go Live with an option for extension for a further period of 3 years 
based on the requirements, specifications, terms, and conditions laid down in this 
tender. 
 
Presently, ATM Switch of Bank is running on NARADA YSE Switch provided by M/s 
Yalamanchilli Software Exports Pvt Ltd (OEM). Debit Card Management system 
(DCMS) is managed by a CBS System Integrator wherein Yalamanchilli Software 
Exports Pvt Ltd is Original Software Developer (OSD) of the Solution. 
 
Bank is looking for a bidder to provide a comprehensive EFT solution, Debit Card 
Management Solution, seamless migration from existing Narada Switch to offer 
switch, complete maintenance of new EFT Switch and Debit Card Management 
solution on under Opex Model on an outsourced basis with following facilities/ 
Services. 
 
The bidder shall perform the role of a system integrator and take accountability of 
end- to-end solution delivery. The Bidder shall design & deliver integrations between 
systems internal to the Bank and support various channels. The bidder shall integrate 
with Bank’s existing services wherever Bank has its own API for any of the FINTECH 
and other services – the responsibility for such integrations will lie with the successful 
bidder. Bidder to design and size the optimal hardware required without
