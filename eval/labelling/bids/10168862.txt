SCOPE OF WORK 
 
AUTOMATIC NUMBER PLATE RECOGNITION (ANPR) AND SPEED DETECTION 
SYSTEM 
 
1.   Overview  
 
Speed Detection & ANPR System across six identified monitoring zones within the MILIT 
campus. All video processing, speed computation, number-plate recognition, event 
generation and monitoring functions shall operate entirely inside MILIT premises, with 
zero external cloud dependency.

3.  Speed Detection & ANPR Software  
 
• Deep-learning object detection combined with multi-object tracking (detector-plus-
tracker architecture) for continuous vehicle trajectories across the camera field of view 
 
 • Camera-calibrated homography mapping converting pixel displacement to real-world 
speed, calibrated against MILIT's actual road geometry at each of the six zones  
 
• Configurable violation thresholds (30–40 km/h band as specified), with confidence-
scored flagging to minimise false positives  
 
• Two-stage ANPR pipeline — plate-localisation network followed by character-
segmentation and recognition  
 
 • OCR tuned specifically for Indian civilian registration-plate formats and fonts 
  
• Confidence-score thresholds applied before a plate read is committed as a violation 
record, with manual-review flagging for low-confidence reads the system is designed 
specifically for multi-zone campus-road environments where several vehicles may be 
tracked simultaneously across each monitored segment.
