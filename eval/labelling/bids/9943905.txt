--- (p.46) Broad Scope of Work for Supply, Implementation, Integration and Maintenance of 
Enterprise AI Platform: 
1. 1. Objective 
The selected Bidder shall be responsible for the design, implementation, integration, deployment, 
operationalization and support of an Enterprise AI Platform, establishment of reusable enterprise AI 
capabilities and implementation of the identified prioritized AI use cases. 
1.1 Enterprise Agentic AI Platform 
The Bank envisages Artificial Intelligence led business transformation founded on a layered 
architecture designed to maximize reusability, governance, interoperability for long-term sustainable 
AI adoption across the organization. 
This layer shall serve as the foundational technology stack for AI initiatives for business transformation 
within the Bank. The proposed platform shall provide centralized and shared services, including but 
not limited to access to Large Language Models (LLMs) and other AI models hosted in public/ 
sovereign cloud that provide high end compute, AI orchestration services, AI governance controls, 
security guardrails, monitoring capabilities, evaluation frameworks, model lifecycle management, 
observability mechanisms and FinOps. 
The bidder shall ensure that this foundational layer provides a standardized, secure and scalable 
environment for the development, deployment and operation of AI solutions across the Bank. 
1.2 Reusable Enterprise AI Capabilities 
Built upon the foundational platform, this layer shall comprise modular, reusable and service-oriented 
AI capabilities that can be consumed by multiple business functions and applications across the 
enterprise. 
The proposed architecture shall promote the development of these capabilities as shared enterprise 
services, thereby reducing duplication of effort, accelerating AI adoption and ensuring consistent 
governance and operational standards.
