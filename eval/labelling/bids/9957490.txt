Section I:Notice Inviting Tender (NIT) 
  
1. National Institute of Ocean Technology (NIOT) under the Ministry of Earth Sciences 
(Govt. of India) manages the Sagar Nidhi, an ice-class ocean going research vessel used 
for carrying out scientific research for the various research programs of Ministry of Earth 
Sciences and other Institutions.   
The Director, National Institute of Ocean Technology (hereinafter referred to as 'the 
Authority' or 'the Procuring Entity'), invites bids for entering into a contract for “Total 
Management Contract of Ocean Research Vessel (ORV) Sagar Nidhi of NIOT” 
(hereinafter referred to as 'the Services) for a period of One year. This NIT is to identify the 
suitable company that are capable of Total Management (hereinafter referred as TM) of the 
vessel including the Operation and Maintenance of Plant and Machineries onboard the 
vessel.
