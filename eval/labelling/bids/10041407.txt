6. Scope of Work 
6.1 Overview 
The scope of this RFP is the supply, installation, implementation, and maintenance 
of Network HSMs (Hardware Security Modules) for the Bank's Aadhaar Data Vault 
(ADV) solution and other Aadhaar-related functionalities. Coordination with the 
ADV Solution Provider for integration of the Network HSMs with the ADV solution. 
A summary of the requirements is set out below. Bidders must submit their 
response/compliance against each item.

comprising:

• 
Two (2) HSMs at the Disaster Recovery (DR) Site
