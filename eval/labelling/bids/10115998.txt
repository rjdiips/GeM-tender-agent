--- (p.16) ANNEXURE I - Broad Scope of Work 
 
1. 1. Project Overview 
The Bank intends to procure, implement, integrate, manage, and maintain an end-to-end MSME 
Digital Lending Platform to enable Straight Through Processing (STP) & Assisted Lending 
Journeys etc. for 3 Years for MSME customers. 
The solution shall support customer acquisition, digital onboarding, eligibility assessment, pre-qual-
ified offer generation, credit decisioning, sanction, documentation, loan account creation, disburse-
ment, servicing, and loan lifecycle management through a seamless omni-channel experience. 
The platform shall support: 
• Pre-Qualified Business Loan (PQBL) and Business Loan journeys (for ETB and NTB Customers) 
• MSME Suite including integration with Government credit linked MSME products, MSME Digital 
STP and Assisted Journeys. For eg. CGSE (Credit Guarantee Scheme for Exporters), Loan for 
Start-ups etc. 
• Any other Digital Journey for MSME as per Bank’s requirement. 
• Customization / Enhancement in existing Digital Journeys of the Bank, as per Business department 
requirements / regulatory requirements. 
The solution shall provide a unified platform accessible through: 
• Responsive Web application 
• Mobile 
• Internet Banking 
• Branch-assisted channels 
• Relationship Manager-assisted journeys 
• QR-based journeys 
• Future digital channels etc. 
The platform shall support end-to-end STP processing wherever eligible while enabling exception 
handling, maker-checker approvals, and assisted processing workflows.
