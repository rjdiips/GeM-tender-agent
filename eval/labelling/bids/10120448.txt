SCOPE OF WORK 
Job Description 
AMC of M/s Cummins Make DG Sets at Pune LPG Plant 
DG Sets Covered 
810 kVA and 250 kVA Cummins Make DG Sets 
Contract Period 
12 Months 
 
A. CONTRACTOR'S SCOPE 
1. The contractor shall provide Annual Maintenance Services for the 810 kVA and 250 kVA Cummins 
make DG sets installed at Pune LPG Plant for a period of 12 months. 
2. The AMC shall include 12 scheduled preventive maintenance visits for each DG set, normally one 
visit per month. The visit schedule shall be finalised mutually after commencement of the contract. 
3. During each scheduled visit, the contractor shall carry out preventive maintenance and inspection of 
the DG sets as per the OEM's standard maintenance check sheet, including but not limited to: 
• 
Checking engine oil, coolant and fuel levels and condition. 
• 
Checking for oil, fuel and coolant leakage. 
• 
Checking batteries, battery terminals, charging system and battery voltage. 
• 
Checking belts, hoses, clamps and associated engine components. 
• 
Inspection of air intake, exhaust and fuel systems. 
• 
Checking engine operating parameters, alarms, indications and safety protections. 
• 
Inspection of alternator and associated electrical connections. 
• 
Inspection/checking of the control panel. 
• 
Checking starting and stopping operation of the DG sets. 
• 
Checking for abnormal noise, vibration, overheating or other abnormalities. 
• 
Functional trial run of the DG sets. 
• 
Review of the DG set logbook/records maintained by BPCL and advising corrective measures, 
wherever required. 
4. B-Check and C-Check shall be carried out as part of the AMC visits whenever they fall due during the 
contract period. 
5. The contractor shall carry out diagnostic checks, fault finding, troubleshooting, adjustments and 
minor repairs/rectification (including arresting minor leakages) during the visits to maintain 
availability of the DG sets.
