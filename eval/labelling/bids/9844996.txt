5.1 Engagement Model 
The successful Bidder (the "System Integrator" or "SI") shall, under a single composite Con-
tract with the Procuring Entity, undertake the supply, installation, configuration, customisa-
tion, testing, training, commissioning and Go-Live Acceptance of an integrated, ABDM-com-
pliant HIMS at KSSSCI. The initial committed award shall include Part A, first-year Part B re-
curring services and first-year Part C FMS. Part A shall include all implementation obliga-
tions and twelve (12) months of warranty / defect-liability support from M5 to M6; first-year 
Part B and Part C shall also operate during that Initial Service Year. After M6, KSSSCI may 
renew Part B and/or Part C on a year-to-year basis, at its sole discretion, for up to nine (9) 
optional annual renewal years, subject to satisfactory performance, budget availability, con-
tinued requirement, approval of the Competent Authority and issuance of a written renewal / 
work order. The maximum supported lifecycle shall not exceed ten (10) years from M5. 
All software licences supplied by the SI shall be perpetual, enterprise-wide, sized to the in-
dicative concurrent-user count specified in Section 1.3, and assignable to KSSSCI on com-
pletion of the engagement. The SI shall not seek any additional licence fee from the Procur-
ing Entity during the term of the Contract on account of growth in registered users, beds, 
OPD footfall or transactional volumes, subject to the upper limits set out in the Bill of Quanti-
ties (Annexure-III).
