--- (p.15) 3.2 Scope of Work 
 
The Bank invites bids & intends to on-board a Service Provider for End-to-End Procurement, 
Implementation, Management and Support of Comprehensive Financial Risk and Compliance 
Solutions as per the scope, specifications, terms and conditions given in detail in this RFP 
document for a period of 5 years. The detailed scope with technical details is illustrated in 
related clauses of RFP. 
 
The selected bidder shall directly provide and implement a comprehensive, scalable 
Comprehensive Financial Risk and Compliance Solutions to the enterprise-level needs of the 
Bank. The solution must ensure high performance, scalability, security, ease of use, and 
seamless integration with existing systems, while supporting future expansions and 
enhancements as per the Bank's requirements. 
 
In MPGB, presently 1320 Branches and other offices (including 21 Regional Offices) are 
handling AML operations in Maker-Checker mode. 
The detailed scope with technical details is covered herein in the RFP. 
The Contract Term / Project period shall be 5 Years since commencement of 
implementation of project. The existing contract may, at the sole discretion of the Bank, be 
extended for an additional period of one year + one year (1+1 year) on the same terms and 
conditions, including commercials.

--- (p.198) (3.3 Broad Scope of Work) 
3.3.1. Broad Scope of Work: 
 
a. Functionalities related to CBS / DMS 
i. 
The solution should have OCR (Optical Character Recognition) functionality 
ii. 
The solution should have Document capture process upon integration with 
Bank’s DMS (Document Management System) 
iii. 
The solution should be able to automatically consume the response files. 
iv. 
The solution should be able to integrate with CBS through API for 
fetching customer details 
 
b. PII Data redaction/Encryption 
 
With RBI keeping strict monitoring over applications capturing customer’s PII data during 
audit, the solution must redact/encrypt the PII before going live. 
c. User Level Escalation Matrix 
 
The solution be able to reflect the escalation matrix, for users ease while checking 
any AML record escalation. 
d. Real Time Data Scrubbing 
 
-The selected bidder must perform the real time data scrubbing on deposits and 
other connected accounts for testing, checking & taking corrective steps with 
regards to KYC compliance. 
 
- All KYC related deficiencies must be addressed within given timelines, including 
correction of mobile numbers. 
e. Dashboard 
 
The AML Software Solution must have a comprehensive dashboard representing 
AMLR record status where user should be able to select the range from dropdowns 
as mentioned below to monitor as and when required: 
 
 Date wise 
 Level wise(branch/RO/HO) 
 Error reason wise 
 TAT Lapsed more than 7 days 
 Dashboard as per regulatory requirements 
 AMLR processed via single search and download 
 AMLR processed via bulk search and download 
 Probable match pendency (Level wise)
