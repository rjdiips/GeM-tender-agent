--- (p.34) Broad Scope of Work 
The Selected Bidder (“Consultant”) is expected to focus on delivery of the assignment in three phases, as defined 
below: 
● 
Phase 1: Design of the Profitability Tracking Framework — Develop a comprehensive operating cost 
segmentation tracking framework for the Bank across vertical, product, scheme and geography 
dimensions, leveraging unit costing methodology and design a well-defined dashboard for reporting 
outcomes. 
● 
Phase 2: Design of the Cost-per-Transaction Framework — Develop a cost-per-transaction framework 
providing visibility into the cost incurred for processing various customer transactions, supported by a well-
defined dashboard for reporting outcomes. 
● 
Phase 3: Implementation Roadmap — Develop a detailed implementation roadmap for institutionalization 
of the Unit Costing and Profitability Tracking Framework across the Bank through a dashboard & specific 
tool design. 
Phase 1: Designing of Profitability Tracking Framework based on Unit Costing 
● 
Obtain a detailed understanding of the Bank's activities across all business verticals, locations and core 
business processes, including enabling and support functions, through Time and Motion Study, interviews 
and survey questionnaires with business verticals and support functions. 
● 
Based on a detailed understanding of the Bank's products and services, map the revenue and cost drivers 
and perform analysis of accounts. 
● 
Develop a granular cost allocation and profitability tracking methodology applicable across the Bank's 
current product offerings, business verticals, locations and channels, as detailed below. 
● 
Design wireframes for automated reports/dashboards providing granular profitability information and 
enabling variance analysis for decision-making.
