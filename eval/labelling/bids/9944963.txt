About the Bank 
Bank of Maharashtra is a public sector bank, formally registered and established on 16.09.1935, in 
Pune, India. Bank is wholly constituted as a body corporate under the Banking Companies 
(Acquisition and Transfer of Undertakings) Act, 1970, with its Head Office at 'Lokmangal', Shivaji 
Nagar, Pune, and its Corporate Office at Montclaire, Baner–Pashan Link Road, Pashan, Pune. The 
Bank operates a three-tier organizational structure comprising Branches, Zonal Offices and the 
Corporate Office. 
The Bank has a nationwide presence of more than 2,800 branches and offices spread across the 
length and breadth of the country. Alongside its domestic franchise, the Bank operates an 
International Banking Unit (IBU) at GIFT City, Gandhinagar, licensed under the International 
Financial Services Centre Authority (IFSCA) framework, extending the Bank's reach into cross-
border and offshore banking, treasury and trade finance business.

4.1. Introduction 
 
Bank invites proposals from eligible bidders to supply, installation and maintenance of AI 
based Full Stack Observability (FSO). This invitation of bids is open to all OEMs/Bidders 
having presence in India to fulfil the minimum qualification criteria as mentioned in RFP 
document. 
 
The Bank proposes to establish a centralized observability and resilience capability at its Data 
Centre and Disaster Recovery Centre, enabling proactive monitoring of its critical applications, 
digital channels, infrastructure and containerized workloads through a Full Stack Observability 
solution with AIOps and GenAI capabilities. The proposed solution shall provide real-time, 
business-centric insight into the performance and health of critical banking applications and 
their underlying infrastructure by leveraging advanced analytics, machine learning, generative 
AI and unified data correlation. 
 
Bank expects the Bidder to deliver proposed solution in-line with the delivery schedule and 
implementation timelines mentioned in Annexure F for “Project Timelines” and technical 
specification and functional requirements mentioned in Annexure H. In-depth scope of work 
is outlined in Annexure C for “Detailed Scope of Work” of this RFP document. Bank seeks 
comprehensive proposals from the bidders who have the capabilities to meet Bank’s 
requirements. Bank reserves the right to further define the Scope of Work through formal 
discussions, formal written communications such as letters/e-mails before execution of SLA 
to that effect, with the successful bidder.

Bank invites proposals from eligible bidders to supply, installation and maintenance of AI 
Based Full Stack Observability (FSO). This invitation of bids is open to all OEMs/Bidders 
having presence in India to fulfil the minimum qualification criteria as mentioned in RFP
