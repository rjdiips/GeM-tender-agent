--- (p.9) 3. SCOPE OF WORK 
The Scope of Work under this Bid Document comprises the design, development, supply, installation, 
integration, commissioning, one (01) year comprehensive operation and maintenance, technical 
support, and dedicated on-site manpower deployment for an AI-Powered Facial Recognition 
Attendance Management System at DIPRUP Headquarters, Soochna Parisar, Lucknow, as detailed 
below. 
3.1 Facial Recognition System Architecture 
• Real-time, AI/Computer-Vision based face recognition deployed across four (04) wall-mounted 
/ kiosk-style, touch-free tablet or terminal setups installed at designated entry/exit points within 
Soochna Parisar, Lucknow. 
• Dual-event tracking with strict Shift-In (morning arrival check-in) and Shift-Out (evening 
departure check-out) recording. 
• Mandatory anti-spoofing / liveness detection to prevent authentication through photograph, 
video, or screen replay spoofing. 
• High-precision recognition performance under diverse indoor lighting conditions, with 
recognition/authentication speed not exceeding one (01) second per employee. 
• Offline buffering capability — attendance terminals shall continue to capture and authenticate 
attendance data during connectivity loss, and shall automatically synchronize buffered data to the 
central cloud server upon restoration of connectivity, without data loss or duplication. 
3.2 Web-Based Central Dashboard & Admin Management System 
• Cloud-hosted, browser-accessible administrative MIS Dashboard for use by the HR / 
Administration Section of DIPRUP. 
• Real-time logging of in/out timestamps, daily attendance rosters, late-arrival and early-departure 
tracking, overtime computation, shift tagging, and automated report generation with export to 
PDF and Excel formats. 
• Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) with distinct access privileges for Admin, Operator, Section 
Head, and Department Executive roles. 
• Complete employee lifecycle management — onboarding, multi-angle facial enrolment,

6. EVALUATION METHODOLOGY (QCBS) 
Evaluation of bids shall be carried out on the basis of the Combined Quality-cum-Cost Based System 
(QCBS), as detailed below. Only those bids which fulfil the Pre-Qualification Eligibility Criteria 
(Section 4) and are found to be substantially responsive (Section 5.3) shall be considered for QCBS 
evaluation. 
• The Technical Proposal shall be assigned a weightage of 70% (Seventy Percent), and the 
Financial Proposal shall be assigned a weightage of 30% (Thirty Percent). 
• The minimum qualifying Technical Score required for consideration of the Financial Bid is 70 
(Seventy) Marks out of 100. Bidders scoring below 70Marks shall be disqualified and their 
Financial Bids shall not be opened. 
• The rate quoted in the Financial Bid must be reasonable and shall remain valid until completion 
of the Project. The validity period may be extended by mutual agreement. 
• The Financial Bid with the lowest quoted cost shall be assigned a Financial Score of 100; the 
Financial Scores of other Bidders shall be computed as inversely proportional to their quoted 
price, as per the formula in Section 6.3 below.
