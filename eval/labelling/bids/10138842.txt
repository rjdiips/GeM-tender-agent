equipment and other infrastructure required for the establishment and operation of the Centralized IEC Unit, as

2
Whether five calling stations are 
required to operate throughout the 
prescribed working hours and 
whether a separate Shift

The requirement under the RFP is for five (5) fully equipped calling stations, each staffed by an IEC Unit Executive. 
The agency shall ensure the availability of all five calling stations throughout the prescribed operating hours, i.e. 
8:00 AM to 8:00 PM daily. The agency may make suitable shift-wise manpower arrangements to ensure 
uninterrupted operations within the quoted consolidated monthly rate. No additional Shift Supervisor/Team Leader 
has been prescribed as a mandatory position under the RFP.
