--- (p.15) 3.2 Scope of Work 
 
The Bank invites bids & intends to on-board a Service Provider for Request for Proposal (RFP) 
for End-to-End Procurement, Implementation, Management and Support of DFIR (Digital 
Forensic & Incident Response) Lab Tools and Services. The detailed scope with technical 
details is illustrated in related clauses of RFP. 
 
The proposed solution and services shall be designed, implemented, and operated to comply with 
the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), along with rules, directions, 
clarifications, and guidance issued by the Government of India, Data Protection Board of India 
(DPBI), MeitY, Reserve Bank of India (RBI), and other Indian statutory or sectoral regulators. 
 
The solution shall adhere to future amendments, rules, and directions issued under the DPDP Act, 
with changes in solution without any additional cost to the Bank, during the contract period 
(including extension period, if any). The solution shall be capable of supporting enhanced 
compliance obligations applicable to a Significant Data Fiduciary (SDF), should the Bank be 
notified as such under the DPDP Act, including additional safeguards, audits, risk assessments, 
and reporting requirements. 
 
The bidder shall provide the services and deliverables including Supply, Installation, Configuration, 
Customization, Implementation, Maintenance and Support of DFIR (Digital Forensic & Incident 
Response) Lab Tools and Services Solution as per Bank's requirement stated in the RFP. 
 
The Contract period / Project period shall be of total of Implementation period and 3 Years from 
the date of go-live of the complete solution. The existing contract may, at the sole discretion of the 
Bank, be extended for an additional period one year + one year (1+1 year), on the same terms & 
conditions, including same commercials, subject to satisfactory performance by the bidder. 
 
The detailed scope with technical details is covered in the Annexure-1 of RFP.
