Scope of Work  
The scope of the work involves following activities. 
 
8.1 Audio-Video Solution 
 
The scope of the work involves following activities. 
 
1. The Bidder/Vendor shall supply, install, integrate, test and commission the Audio-Video Solution 
for Conference Room, Central Office at Nariman Point, Mumbai and also maintain the solution 
during warranty period of 3 years and post warranty, 2 years under comprehensive Annual 
Maintenance Contract. 
2. The contract includes all components including all the devices, parts & accessories. Installation & 
Integration of necessary Devices and Firmware updates/upgrades/security patches / software 
version upgrades to be done wherever required to complete the solution. 
3. Quarterly preventive maintenance of equipment/solution during warranty period of 3 years and 
under the 2 years comprehensive Annual Maintenance Contract. The report for the same should 
be submitted for all the devices / machines / equipment under contract. 
4. The vendor shall train the Bank’s existing support persons/engineers/staff at central office, 
Mumbai, in all respects to ensure that the Audio-Video solution can be used smoothly without any 
errors. 
5. The proposed Audio-Video solution must have the capability of seamless integration with the 
existing Cisco Video Conferencing infrastructure already installed in the Bank. 
6. The HDMI/USB-C switcher that will be used in the solution, must be placed below the conference 
room table itself, concealed/hidden from plain sight to avoid loss of signal due to use of long USB-
C/HDMI cables. 
7. The VC Codec, Automation Controller and Power Amplifier, must be placed on the rack provided 
behind the Main Display with good ventilation. 
8. The Bidder must resolve any hardware, system software and integration issues with existing VC 
infrastructure and application related problems during installation of the complete Audio-Video 
Solution.
