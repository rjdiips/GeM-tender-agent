This RFP seeks a qualified vendor to provide a comprehensive, analytics-driven Centralized 
Monitoring Unit (CMU) solution for proactive monitoring of customer master data quality, KYC 
compliance and account maintenance controls across the Bank’s channels of customer acquisition, 
digital (e-platform), Branches through Zonal Centralized Account-opening Departments (Z-CODs) 
and Business Correspondents (BCs). The solution should enhance the Bank’s ability to identify, 
track and remediate anomalies in customer master data and KYC records, and ensure compliance 
with regulatory requirements such as those of the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) and the Financial 
Intelligence Unit (FIU). The broad scope is divided into three parts: 
a) Implementation of the Centralized Monitoring Unit (CMU) analytics solution, an image and 
document analytics tool, and technical support. 
b) Historical data validation to verify gaps in the Bank’s existing customer master records to 
facilitate remediation. 
c) Consultancy and managed services for setting up and running the Centralized Monitoring 
Unit, including establishment of a Centre of Excellence (CoE).

--- (p.192) 2. Broad Scope of Work 
The Bidder shall provide an end to end solution together with consultancy services and an 
onsite support team, on a Build, Operate and Transfer (BOT) basis, comprising the following: 
 Set up of a dedicated Centralised Monitoring Unit with clear roles and responsibilities, 
escalation mechanism and governance. 
 Supply, implementation, integration, customisation, commissioning and support of an 
image and document analytics solution that automates the monitoring of customer 
master data quality and KYC compliance. 
 Development, testing and deployment of the validation and anomaly detection rules, on 
the basis of the Bank's Board approved policies and SOPs, regulatory guidelines and 
expectations, and industry practice. 
 Generation of observations on a T+1 basis, or at such other frequency as the Bank may 
define, with a structured evidence pack, and routing of the observations through a case 
management workflow to the CMU and the concerned operating units for remediation. 
 Configuration of dashboards, management information and role based access for the 
CMU, the operating units and senior management. 
 Integration of the solution with the Bank's Core Banking System, Document 
Management System, Data Warehouse, APIs ,CKYC Registry (CERSAI),etc. 
 One time historical data validation run covering the existing customer base, addressing 
legacy data complexities including the non availability of historical KYC documents in 
the current Document Management System, the phased availability of historical KYC 
records, and validation against Core Banking System data where evidence documents 
are not retrievable.
