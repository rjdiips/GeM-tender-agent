Scope of Work 
 
NABARD seeks an Enterprise KYC, KYB, CKYC & AML Solution for development, 
implementation, integration and roll-out across NABARD applications and systems. 
The Solution shall provide complete end-to-end capabilities for Individual KYC, Entity 
KYB, CKYC registry integration and lifecycle management, and AML/CFT compliance, 
screening and transaction monitoring. The Bidder shall be responsible for the design, 
development, supply, configuration, customization, integration, testing, deployment, 
security hardening, Go-Live and ongoing support of the complete Solution. 
 
For the purpose of this Scope of Work, “Enterprise KYC, KYB, CKYC and AML 
Solution” or “Solution” shall mean the complete integrated platform comprising 
Individual KYC, Entity KYB, CKYC registry integration and lifecycle management, and 
AML/CFT capabilities specified herein. 
 
The solution shall provide due diligence capabilities including customer acceptance, 
customer identification procedure, customer due diligence, beneficial owner 
identification (for entities), ongoing due diligence triggers, automated regulatory 
compliance flows, and audit ready evidence and reporting as per regulatory and Bank’s 
KYC/AML policy. 
 
1. Standards based Integration:  
The Bidder shall provide API/Web-SDK or other applicable standards-based 
integration so that NABARD applications (web portals, internal systems, and 
authentication services such as Active Directory/SSO where applicable) can invoke 
KYC/KYB/CKYC services. Integration shall support initiation, status retrieval, 
decision outcomes, evidence retrieval, and master record updates.

modularity, scalability, interoperability, and reusability across NABARD 
systems.
