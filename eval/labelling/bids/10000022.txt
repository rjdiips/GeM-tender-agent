Broad Scope of Work of the “Proposed Trade Finance Solution” 
 
The Bank intends to procure an Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning 
(ML) powered, centralized enterprise Trade Finance Solution i.e., ―FXPC‖ solution. 
The core objective is to achieve Straight Through Processing (STP). The solution 
will automate the end-to-end transaction lifecycle across a multi-entity 
environment through intelligent document processing and integrated multi-
platform connectivity.  
 
Description of the envisaged scope is enumerated below . The bidder needs to 
quote accordingly in the Commercial Sheet mentioned in Annexure XV.  
 
The Bidder needs to consider and envisage all services that would be required in 
the Scope and ensure the same is delivered to the Bank. The Bank will not 
accept any plea of the Bidder later for omission of services on the pretext that 
the same was not explicitly mentioned in the RFP. 
 
The proposed solution is to be implemented for Front Office Layer (Customer / 
Branch) for request initiation/generation and Mid Office Layer for assessment, 
compliance and fulfilment. Mid office layer of function will be undertaken at 
controlling office at respective centre or any centre as per Bank‘s requirement. 
Back-office layer for processing, sanctioning, reporting, trade settlement, CBS 
accounting, charges, GST invoices and SWIFT transmission and others. 
 
Bidder should have a comprehensive migration plan for all outstanding legacy 
transactions from CBS.
