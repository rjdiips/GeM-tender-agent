--- (p.77) A. Scope of Work: 
The brief scope of work for the bidder is to provide, implement, and manage the Bank's 
Domain Name System (DNS) Services (Authoritative and Recursive), along with 
associated support services, through a shared service model hosted in the bidder's 
own data centres, co-located facilities, or MeitY-empanelled cloud service provider 
data centres. 
The services covered as part of Bidder should include following but not limited in any 
manner. If any services, functions or responsibilities not specifically described in this 
scope but are an inherent, necessary or customary part of the services and are 
required for proper performance or provision of the services in accordance with the 
scope, Bidder shall be deemed to include such services, functions or responsibilities 
not specifically described at no additional cost to the Bank. 
1. Purpose and Objective 
This document outlines the scope of work, roles and responsibilities, and service 
expectations for the provision and management of the Bank's Domain Name System 
(DNS) Services (Authoritative and Recursive). The DNS infrastructure plays a vital role 
in supporting the Bank's digital ecosystem by resolving domain names into IP 
addresses and enabling uninterrupted access to internet-facing and internal services. 
The proposed solution shall ensure high availability, security, performance, resilience, 
and operational efficiency in line with the Bank's business and regulatory 
requirements. 
The DNS Management Solution shall encompass robust capabilities for control, policy 
enforcement, 
compliance 
management, 
monitoring, 
visibility, 
administration, 
reporting, and alerting. The requirements outlined below are indicative and not 
exhaustive, and the solution shall include all necessary features and functionalities 
required to meet the Bank's operational, security, and regulatory requirements.
