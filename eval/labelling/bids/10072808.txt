This Section sets out the objective and purpose of the procurement (Clause 1) and the Scope of Work

1. Objective and Purpose 
1.1 Background 
The Bank has undertaken a structured programme to strengthen its Information Systems Audit 
function in line with the Reserve Bank of India (Commercial Banks – Cybersecurity, Technology: Risk, 
Resilience and Assurance Framework) Directions, 2026, issued on 31 July 2026 under reference 
RBI/DoS/2026-27/410. As part of this programme, the Bank has established a three-lines-of-defence 
operating model, a Common Control Framework, and an updated IS Audit Policy, Standard Operating 
Procedure that together govern how IS audits are planned, executed, reported and tracked. With this 
foundation in place, the Bank now intends to automate its IS audit lifecycle. The present process relies 
on manual validation across multiple tools, manual comparison of reported positions against actuals, 
and limited ability to track repeat observations, correlate data across audits, or maintain a dynamic 
view of risk. The Bank has completed the preparatory groundwork for automation, including 
identification of the audit processes in scope, a catalogue of automation opportunities across the audit 
lifecycle, an evaluation of available solutions, and a data plan built on a tool-agnostic audit data 
repository with analytics-based prioritisation and AI-assisted capabilities subject to mandatory human 
review. Through this RFP, the Bank seeks a right-fit solution and implementation partner that will
