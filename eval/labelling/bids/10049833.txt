--- (p.35) BROAD SCOPE OF WORK 
The Bank is issuing this Request for Proposal (RFP) to solicit proposals from qualified vendors 
to transition our existing cybersecurity infrastructure from an on-premises SIEM deployment 
to a next-generation, cloud-native, SaaS-based SIEM platform. As the Bank accelerates its 
digital transformation, our expanding infrastructure demands a highly scalable, flexible, and 
resilient security monitoring architecture capable of unifying visibility across hybrid cloud 
environments, containers, APIs, and modern digital assets. To achieve this, the Bank requires 
a single, comprehensive solution, hereafter referred to as the SaaS SIEM platform. 
 
Strategic Objectives & Scope 
The purpose of this deployment is to modernize our Security Operations Center (SOC) by 
preserving foundational features while introducing cutting-edge, AI-driven, and big data-
enabled real-time analytics. 
The selected platform must deliver the following: 
AI Assisted SOC 
The platform must provide AI-powered natural language processing for plain-language 
queries, advanced AI-assisted threat hunting to uncover hidden adversary behaviours, and 
automated alert stitching that correlates disparate security events into clear, chronological 
attack chains. 
User Behaviour Analytics (UEBA) 
The solution must continuously monitor and baseline the typical activities of all users and 
devices to detect deviations, utilize machine learning to automatically parse and map diverse 
log formats without manual regular expressions, and flag high-risk anomalies such as 
compromised accounts and insider threats. This monitoring must extend not only to individuals
