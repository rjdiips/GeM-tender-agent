--- (p.14) 2. Scope of Work 
Stockholding proposes to deploy software defined wide area network (SD WAN) for its Data 
Center, Disaster Recovery Site (Colocation) and its branch offices across India. Apart from SD-
WAN implementation, Stockholding also proposes to replace Branch switches across all its 
branch offices in India. The bidder is required to integrate the existing MPLS, ILL and local 
broadband lines at all the locations into the SD WAN solution in conformity with the technical 
specifications (Annexure 10 – Technical Specifications). 
 
3. Current Architecture 
1) MPLS Connectivity – Presently, Head Offices/DR/Branches of Stockholding are 
connected through a MPLS (WAN) provided by BSNL and Tata Communications (TCL) 
(Alternate). BSNL is the primary service provider for Stockholding while the secondary 
link is being managed by TCL. The secondary link may or may not be via MPLS Link. 
Branch Offices on MPLS to Main DC are connected via 2 Mbps bandwidth. 
 
2) Local Broadband Connectivity – Since primary link of all branches connected to 
DC/DR is via MPLS link, many branch offices are also having broadband connections as 
secondary link. 
 
3) Internet Connectivity and Traffic Management: To enable branch internet and 
application access at each Branch Office, the traffic routes to DC site through DC firewall 
and proxy via MPLS Link. Traffic for Internal applications accessed over WAN are also 
routed to DC via MPLS link.

Routers (4 in DC Site and 2 in DR Site) where both primary and secondary internet links 
terminate. Both DC and DR Sites are in HA Configuration (1+1). At DC Site, there are 2 
routers one for each line of business in HA Mode.
