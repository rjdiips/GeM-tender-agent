--- (p.17) Scope of Work 
5.1. 
General 
5.1.1. The bidder shall supply, implement, configure, and support an enterprise-grade API 
Gateway solution through the GeM (Government e-Marketplace) procurement 
process, meeting the Bank’s requirements for a secure, scalable, resilient, and 
governed API ecosystem. The proposed solution shall be industry standards, fully 
supported by the OEM, and deployable on on-premises, cloud, or hybrid models as 
per the Bank’s infrastructure guidelines. The solution shall comply with all applicable 
regulatory and supervisory requirements, including guidelines issued by RBI, and 
adhere to enterprise-grade information security standards. 
5.1.2. The Bidder shall implement and maintain the API Management (APIM) platform 
across five (5) environments: Development (DEV), User Acceptance Testing (UAT), 
Pre-Production (PRE-PROD), Production (PROD), and Disaster Recovery (DR). The 
UAT environment shall support both integration and business validation testing. The 
PRE-PROD environment shall closely mirror the PROD environment for final 
validation, performance testing, and release readiness. The PROD environment shall 
host live APIs, while the DR environment shall ensure business continuity and 
disaster recovery. The solution shall support secure API promotion across 
environments through automated CI/CD pipelines, role-based access control, 
monitoring, logging, and compliance with the Bank's security and regulatory 
requirements. 
5.1.3. The bidder shall be responsible for end-to-end implementation of the API 
management platform, including installation, configuration, and commissioning 
across Implementation, Migration, Testing, Sandbox, and Production environments. 
A dedicated sandbox environment shall be provided to enable safe testing and 
onboarding of internal teams and external vendors without impacting production 
systems. The bidder shall also implement a Disaster Recovery (DR) setup in active-
passive mode with appropriate data synch
