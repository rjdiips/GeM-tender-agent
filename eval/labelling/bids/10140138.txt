--- (p.29) BROAD SCOPE OF WORK FOR “Engagement of Consultant for Strategic 
Technology Transformation” 
The scope of “Engagement of Consultant for Strategic Technology Transformation” shall 
encompass: 
 • Transformation (Digital & Workforce): Formulation of the Five-Year IT Strategy Roadmap, 
Enterprise Architecture, application modernization strategy, AI & GenAI adoption framework, 
workforce capability roadmap, SDLC transformation, Quantum-Safe, and technology modernization 
initiatives. 
• Risk, Compliance & Governance: Assessment of existing Cyber Security Operation Centre 
(CSOC) and Cyber Security Centre of Excellence (CcoE) and formulation of Transformation of 
Cyber Security Operation Centre into Development of the AI Led Cyber Fusion Centre (CISO 
2.0), strengthening CcoE, developing Enterprise IT & AI Risk Management Framework, 
governance models, cybersecurity strategy, regulatory alignment, and risk monitoring 
mechanisms. 
• Improved Efficiency & Resiliency: Assessment and enhancement of application and infrastructure 
resiliency, observability, capacity planning, data centre modernization, operational resilience 
frameworks, and technology governance practices. 
• Value Creation & Customer Delight: Enablement of AI-driven innovation, data-driven decision-
making, enhanced digital customer journeys, analytics capabilities, and modernization initiatives 
aimed at improving customer experience and business outcomes. 
• Ease of Doing Business: Enterprise data management transformation, process simplification, 
automation, standardization, governance enhancements, and the establishment of future-ready 
technology platforms that support operational efficiency and faster decision-making.
