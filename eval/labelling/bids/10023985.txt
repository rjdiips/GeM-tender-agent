SCOPE OF WORK & TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS
Anti-Termite Treatment & Pest Control Services for Central Library
Location: Central Library, Selection Centre
Central, Bhopal
Total Area: 995 Sq. Ft.
Estimated Value: ₹ 24,750/-
Warranty Required: Minimum 1 Year
1. WORK DESCRIPTION
Execution of comprehensive chemical Anti-Termite Treatment and General Pest Control targeting
masonry walls, wall-floor joints, iron almirahs, wooden furniture, and paper assets across the 995 Sq.
Ft. premise of Central Library, Selection Centre Central, Bhopal.
2. DETAILED SCOPE OF WORK
Wall & Structural Joint Treatment: Chemical injection, precision drilling, and pressure spraying
along all interior and exterior masonry walls, wall-floor junctions, plaster cracks, and skirting lines
to create an unbroken anti-termite chemical barrier.
Iron Almirahs & Steel Racks Protection: Inspection and chemical barrier treatment around and
beneath all Iron Almirahs and Steel Racks to isolate steel storage from subterranean termite
entry.
Wooden Furniture & Fixtures: Deep chemical spraying and localized treatment of wooden tables,
chairs, door/window frames, wooden bookshelves, and wooden fittings.
Protection of 8,000+ Books & Paper Assets: Special care using non-staining, approved
chemicals to protect over 8,000 library books, documents, registers, and cataloging materials
without causing chemical staining or moisture damage.
Chemical Specifications: Exclusive use of CIB&RC-approved, low-odor/odorless chemicals (e.g.,
Imidacloprid / Chlorpyrifos or equivalent industry-standard formulations).
3. TERMS & CONDITIONS
Warranty / Guarantee: Minimum 1 Year comprehensive warranty from the date of
completion. Free re-treatment within 48 hours of notification if termites reappear on walls,
furniture, or paper assets during the warranty period.
Safety & Environmental Standards: All applied chemicals must be eco-friendly, non-staining,
and approved by the Central Insecticides Board & Registration Committee (CIB&RC).
